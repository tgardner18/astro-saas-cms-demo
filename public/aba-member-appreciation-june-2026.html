June is Member Appreciation Month. Thank you for being part of ABA.

June 2026 · All Month Long

Enter for a Chance to Win: Member Appreciation Giveaways

ABA is celebrating member banks and the talented people who power them. Enter any — or all — of our five giveaways before June 30, 2026. Winners will be announced the first week of July.

GIVEAWAY 1: Attend a Webinar or Download a Featured Resource

Prize: 4 winners · $250 Amazon Gift Card each

  • Register for and attend any ABA webinar in June, or download one of ABA's featured research reports during the month
  • Confirm your member bank affiliation at registration — you're automatically entered
Read contest rules and conditions »

GIVEAWAY 2: Banking Innovation Challenge

Prize: 2 winners · Full Annual Convention registration + 2 nights hotel (est. $1,400 value each)

  • Submit a 150–300 word description of how your bank used technology or AI to better serve customers in the past year
  • Submissions reviewed by an ABA panel; winners notified by July 11, 2026
Read contest rules and conditions »

GIVEAWAY 3: ABA Banking Trivia Challenge

Prize: 8 winners · ABA Member Merch Bundle each

  • Log in with your ABA member credentials and complete a 10-question quiz on banking history, regulation, and ABA milestones
  • Members who score 7 or more correct answers are entered in the random draw
Read contest rules and conditions »

GIVEAWAY 4: Community Impact Story Contest

Prize: 4 winners · Feature in ABA Banking Journal + $500 donation to a nonprofit of your choice

  • Submit a 200–400 word story about how your bank supported your local community — affordable housing, financial literacy, small business lending, and more
  • Entries may be used for ABA communications with your permission
Read contest rules and conditions »

GIVEAWAY 5: "Day in the Life" Photo Contest

Prize: 4 winners · $500 Gift Card each

  • Post a photo on LinkedIn or Instagram that captures the culture and spirit of your bank using #ABAAppreciates and tag @ABABankers
  • Include your bank's name in the caption; the four most engaging posts win
Read contest rules and conditions »

Thank you to all members for participating — we look forward to reading your entries!

June-Only Benefits

Maximize Your Membership This Month

In addition to our five giveaways, ABA is unlocking six special benefits for member bank employees throughout June 2026. Use ABA's Member Toolkit to make the most of your membership all year long.

  • 1
    25% Off All Conference Registrations
    Register for any ABA conference in June — Annual Convention, Risk & Compliance, Bank Marketing, and more — and receive 25% off the standard member rate.
  • 2
    Three Free Premium Research Downloads
    ABA's 2026 AI in Banking Survey, Community Bank CEO Priorities, and the Consumer Payments Outlook report are all unlocked at no cost for members in June.
  • 3
    Members-Only Ask-an-Expert Webinar · June 18
    Join ABA subject matter experts live for Q&A covering regulatory outlook for 2H 2026, AI governance frameworks, and commercial real estate conditions.
  • 4
    Free Certification Exam Prep Session
    Preparing for a CRCM, CTFA, CAFP, or CERP? Attend a complimentary group prep webinar with ABA-certified instructors this June. Seating is limited.
  • 5
    Member Exchange Open House Month
    All 13 ABA Member Exchanges are hosting open-house events in June. Invite a colleague who hasn't joined yet — they can observe and participate for free all month.
  • 6
    Your Personalized ABA Resource Kit
    Complete a 3-minute survey about your role and ABA will email you a curated toolkit of resources, connections, and tools matched to your daily responsibilities.
